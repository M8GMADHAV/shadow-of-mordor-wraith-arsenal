## 🛡️ Engineered for Stability & Fidelity

We understand that the primary concern with any gameplay enhancement tool is the stability of the host process. The `Liki-js/shadow-of-mordor-internal-trainer` architecture prioritizes **memory safety** and **clean unload routines**.

We leverage a manual mapping technique that integrates directly into the game's main thread, allowing for synchronized data callbacks. This design minimizes the risk of desynchronization errors and crash-inducing conflicts with the game’s standard operations. The system is designed to sit quietly until called upon, ensuring your fps remains unshackled and your graphics settings can be pushed to 'Ultra' without penalty.

### 🔧 Troubleshooting & FAQ

**Q: My game crashes on startup when the trainer is loaded.**
**A:** Ensure you are running the exact GOTY version of the game. Verify your game files via Steam to ensure none are corrupted. Also, please disable any third-party framerate limiters (e.g., RivaTuner) as they can conflict with the internal injector.

**Q: The overlay appears, but the hotkeys do not work.**
**A:** Your antivirus or Windows Defender may be blocking the virtual input driver. Please add an exception for the `.dll` file (see *src/injector/*). The tool requires a standard driver for keyboard hooks.

**Q: Is there a risk of a VAC ban?**
**A:** No. Since the game does not use Valve Anti-Cheat software (it is a single player game), there are no server-side authentication checks. The risk of a "ban" is entirely theoretical and limited to your local account.
